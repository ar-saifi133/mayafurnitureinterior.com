About Us

Introduction

Welcome to our world of turnkey interior solutions, where we seamlessly blend creativity, functionality, and efficiency to craft spaces that exceed your wildest imagination. Our commitment to excellence and attention to detail make us your trusted partner in bringing your interior dreams to life.

Maya Furniture & Interior deals in commercial projects in a broad range of styles and sensibilities. It was founded as a modest firm named M.S.INDUSTRIES in early 2002 by Mr. Hussain Saifi and later known as Maya Furniture & Interior. We have our headquarter in Delhi and reach across the country.

With years of experience in the industry, we have honed our skills to perfection. Our team of talented designers, architects, and craftsmen collaborate harmoniously to deliver turnkey solutions that reflect your unique style and vision. From concept to completion, we handle every aspect with precision.

Be it end-to-end interiors, renovation or modular solutions, we have it all for your home or office. With a wide range of furniture & decor, we have your back from start to finish.

Contact us today, and let's embark on a journey to create a space that truly defines you. Your dream interior is just a step away!
